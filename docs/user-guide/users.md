# Users and logging in

Every page needs you to log in with your email and password. Sessions last 12 hours.

Any logged-in user can manage users on the **Users** page:

- **Add a user** with their first and last name, email and a password of at least 10 characters.
- **Delete** a user. They are logged out straight away. You can't delete your own account, so
  there is always someone who can log in.

Your name and the app's version number are shown at the right of the top bar, with **Log out**.

**Docs** in the top bar opens this documentation inside the app. The address bar follows the page
you are reading, so you can bookmark or share it, and **Open in a new tab** shows it on its own.

The first user is created from the command line; see [Installation](../deployment/installation.md#create-the-first-user).
